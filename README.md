# BrewCraft Content Assistant — Custom GPT Hands-On Topic 15

## Topic

Topic 15 — Building Marketing Strategy & Content Assistant GPTs

## Project

BrewCraft Content Assistant

## Objective

Build a brand-safe Marketing Strategy and Content Assistant that creates
marketing content for BrewCraft Coffee while:

- Collecting required campaign inputs before generating content
- Maintaining a warm and passionate-barista persona
- Following verified BrewCraft brand guidelines
- Using only approved factual claims
- Blocking unsupported marketing hype
- Blocking prohibited health and medical claims
- Maintaining the required Headline → Body Copy → CTA structure
- Providing safe alternatives when requested wording violates the guidelines
